Weak scaling measures how the performance of the model evolves when the problem size grows proportionally to the number of processing units, so that the workload per processing unit is kept constant. Every case increases both the resolution and the number of processors, and the first case is taken as the reference.

The section presents a comparison table, with one row per case, and a comparison plot of the throughput of every case. In the ideal case the throughput stays constant as the problem size and the resources grow together, which would mean that the model scales perfectly. A decreasing trend indicates that the larger cases spend a relatively larger share of their time in communication or in serial work.
