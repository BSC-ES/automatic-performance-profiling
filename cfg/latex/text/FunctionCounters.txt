The table collects the hardware counters of the tracked user functions, one row per function and traced case. The columns are the PAPI ids of the counters: IPC is the instructions per cycle, TOT\_CYC the cycles, L1\_DCM and L2\_DCM the level 1 and level 2 data cache misses per 1000 instructions, and L3\_TCM the level 3 total cache misses per 1000 instructions, so the cache values are rates and not counts. Each figure plots one of those counters against the number of MPI tasks of the traced cases, with one line per function.
