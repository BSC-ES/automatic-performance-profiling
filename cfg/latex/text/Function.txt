This section reports the user functions instrumented in the trace. It starts with the ranking of the most time-consuming functions and the share of the fastest timestep that each of them takes, and then presents, for every component, two tables with one row per user function and traced case: the first splits the time spent inside the function into its MPI calls, and the second gives its IPC, the cycles and the cache misses per thousand instructions. Each table is followed by its figure: a stacked bar per function and case for the MPI profile, and the evolution of the IPC and of the L3 misses along the cases. The per-function time scalability tables close the section.

The purpose is to identify the functions that dominate the runtime and to see whether they scale as the number of processing units grows. A function whose time per timestep does not decrease, or whose share of the total time grows, with more processors is a candidate bottleneck, and the MPI and hardware counter columns of its row help to tell whether the cause is communication or computation.
